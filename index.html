SYSTEM_LOG // JOURNEY.LOG

The Background

My obsession with the virtual world began in childhood, but it evolved into a serious discipline during my first year of high school when I started taking formal courses. In my second year, a mentor in my robotics club gave me a piece of advice that shifted my perspective entirely: "Do not focus solely on programming; master robotics as a whole." That moment sparked my drive to bridge the gap between abstract code and physical hardware.

Since then, my education has been a self-driven pursuit of technical excellence. Graduating from Harvard's CS50 was more than an academic milestone; it gave me the architectural foundation to solve real-world problems. Currently, I am deep-diving into modern full-stack web development through The Odin Project. I constantly work on side projects to sharpen my skills, extending even to the hardware level, having recently built my own PC from scratch to ensure total understanding of my computing environment.

A major focus of mine has been addressing the limited access to STEM resources in Greece—a reality made clear when there is only one shop in an entire state for electronic components. To combat this, my CS50 final project was stemnet.gr. I built a self-sustaining, alumni-driven platform with a secure SQL database handling user authentication, posts, and affiliate networking. It is designed so Greek students can find out-of-school academic resources, post upcoming events, and find the guidance I once had to seek out myself.
